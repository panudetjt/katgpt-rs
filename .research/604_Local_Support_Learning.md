## TL;DR

Catastrophic forgetting is reframed as a **geometric problem in the input space of each weight matrix**: a gradient update `ΔW = -α·(dL/dv)·xᵀ` alters the mapping of EVERY input not orthogonal to the training activation `x` — `Δv_pre ∝ x(s)ᵀ·x_pre ≠ 0` — which is why every optimizer (Adam rescales, Muon orthogonalizes, both still global) is "suboptimal" under the retention objective. The fix is **locality**: pair each weight matrix's LoRA adapter with a **gate that opens only on activations from the adapter's own training distribution**. The gate is a **two-GMM likelihood ratio** — `Φ_pos` fit (EM) on current-phase input activations, `Φ_neg` fit ONCE on ~1M tokens of a *generic* reference corpus (FineWeb-Edu; need not match actual pretraining data — it must capture distribution **width, not identity**); adapter applies iff `Φ_pos(x) > Φ_neg(x)`. Per-token, per-matrix, EMA-smoothed over the token stream. Result on Qwen2.5-1.5B/3B/7B-Instruct: full-capacity learning with **96.6% → 98.8% retention** (smoothing increment) across 3 sequential phases; LoRA baseline loses 44% after ONE phase; hyperparameter-robust (rank/lr/batch no longer trade against forgetting); gates cost **13.8 MB total (6.9 MB/phase)** vs a 14 GB model; ×1.64 training time; +86 ms/forward at 7B (unoptimized).

**Why it matters to us:** the entire gate plane is **modelless** — EM statistics, a seeded JL projection, a closed-form O(K·E) log-likelihood ratio, an EMA scalar, and an offline-certifiable excess bound (App E). It is the **continuous-latent mixture-density member of our shipped gate family** — the family that today has: identity guard, relevance gates, `CorpusDistanceGate` (one-sided exemplar cosine), `contrastive_scope` (two-corpus NB log-LLR on *token counts*), and the per-region Gaussian gates of `RegionSubspaceField` (shared Ψ, no mixture density, no EM). Nothing in the stack fits a **continuous-latent two-density mixture ratio over activations with per-component variances**, and nothing gates **adapter application by input support** (`LoRAHotSwap` dispatches by explicit `{npc_id, context_hash}` key, never by the activation itself). ⚠ Cost honesty (fixed at verdict): the JL projection D×E per token is the SAME ORDER as the gated multiply it precedes (at D=1536/E=256: ~1× Qwen2.5-1.5B k_proj, which is 1536×256 for 2 KV heads × head_dim 128 — verdict round 2 correction from an earlier "~2×" off a wrong 1536×128 figure), and two GMM evals at K=32/E=256 are ~50–64K FLOPs — per-matrix adapter gating costs ~11–16% of the gated path per matrix (q/k/v gate-sharing ~11–12%, o_proj ~16%, down_proj ~15–16%), consistent with the paper's +86 ms/forward at 7B, NOT sub-percent; the cheap consumer class is per-REQUEST gates (reflex abstain, healer scope: one projection + one eval per request). Per-matrix cost bars are therefore asserted at LAYER or FORWARD-PASS scope, never per single matrix.

## Core mechanism (verbatim-shape digest)

```
Training (per phase i):   Φ_neg ← FitGMMs(M, D_ref)              [once; generic corpus]
                          W_adapter(i) ← FitAdapters(M, D_i, E)  [standard LoRA; gate ALWAYS OPEN in training]
                          Φ_pos(i) ← FitGMMs(M ⊕ W_adapter(i), D_i)
Inference (per token x, per module i):
                          ℓ(x) = log Φ_pos(i)(x) − log Φ_neg(x)  [diag-cov GMM, JL→256 dims]
                          s ← α·1[ℓ>0] + (1−α)·s                 [EMA temporal smoothing]
                          v ← v + 1[s>0.5] · W_adapter(i)·x
```

Key design facts (paper-verified):

- **JL projection to E=256 + diagonal covariance** turns the naive `O(PLKD²)` gate into `O(PBL(DE+KE))` — the D² killer removed. K=16–32 components; **insensitive down to K=2** (base-model representations do the work).
- **Φ_neg is shared** across all phases and matrices of the same shape class; gates shared across matrices with identical inputs (q/k/v) → **−43% gate count**.
- **Theory (App E) — the PAPER's own wording, quoted for the record; our implementable form differs (verdict rounds 1–2):** a gate's error vs the worst-case prior distribution decomposes into **deficit** (closes on in-phase data — fixed by the training objective) and **excess** (opens off-support — THE forgetting channel). Any objective that is a P-weighted average is **blind to excess** (P has no mass there); whatever controls it comes from the **hypothesis class**. In the paper's continuous formulation: `vol(excess) ≤ ‖P̂−P‖₁/τ`, and with the paper's **input-dependent threshold τ(x)=Φ_neg(x)** the bound needs only `τ_min > 0` (Scott & Nowak minimum-volume set estimation, their α=1 case). ⚠ Our shipped form: Lebesgue volume is unmeasurable under unbounded Gaussians in 256-D, so the stack's validator checks **Φ_neg-weighted excess MASS** (sample the reference, measure the open rate) against the ε predicted from fixture-known P only (Plan 618 T6/T7) — never real-corpus volume claims.
- **Ablations that are design priors for us:** (a) **MLP discriminator gates FAIL OOD** — discriminative classes open arbitrarily outside training data; density classes stay closed by construction (supports our NB-LLR/`contrastive_scope` choice over trained classifiers, and motivates the density-class sibling); (b) **per-matrix gates ≫ single router** (single embedding-level gate recovers only 50–77% of the gain, and propagates one gate's error to every adapter); (c) **UoS (union-of-spheres exemplar support) ≈ GMM retention but 5.67 GB vs 13.8 MB** — locality is the active ingredient; parametric density is the *memory compression* of exemplar support; (d) **GMM ablation carries the retention: 76.6% → 96.6%**, EMA adds 96.6% → 98.8%; (e) OP-LoRA-class orthogonal-subspace methods are refuted at scale: a standard LoRA update puts **≤1.7% of its energy in the top-512 singular subspace** of the pretrained weights — forgetting is not a weight-space-subspace problem.

## Novelty gate (pinned claim, scoped)

**Pinned claim (restated at verdict round 1):** a *per-matrix per-token adapter-admission gate and request-level abstention primitive* built on a **continuous-latent two-density log-ratio** (diagonal-covariance GMM pair with per-component variances, EM-fit, JL-projected) plus an **offline excess/deficit certification instrument** — distinguished from `contrastive_scope` (discrete token-count NB ratio, document-level, no density fit, no per-matrix position) by the continuous-latent mixture density + adapter-gating position + certification; from `RMD` (single shared-covariance Gaussian pair, eval-time OOD scoring) by mixture density + serving/adapter-gating position + certification; from `CorpusDistanceGate` by two-sided parametric density vs one-sided exemplars; and from the stack's `RegionSubspaceField` by per-component variances + a true logsumexp mixture density + EM + the pos/neg pairing. (The earlier "input-dependent threshold τ(x)" framing is RETIRED: ℓ>0 is a fixed threshold on the ratio, the same shape as contrastive_scope's fixed θ — the reviewer is right; τ(x) remains a valid contrast only against one-sided gates.)

**Published prior art (§4 searches, 2 subagents, ~31 queries): NARROWS — no kill.** The exact composition is unpublished; the components are not, and the claim must be scoped:

- **RMD** (Ren et al., AISTATS 2022, arXiv:2106.09022) — two-Gaussian log-density ratio on hidden states; closest structural ancestor; eval-time, single global threshold.
- **LR-OOD** (Ren et al., NeurIPS 2019) — pos/background density ratio with generic reference model; eval-time scoring.
- **Heng & Soh** (arXiv:2505.15008, ICLR 2026) — Neyman–Pearson likelihood-ratio optimal acceptance for selective classification; the *rule* is prior art; not the GMM-over-activations construction, not a serving gate.
- **HME** (Jacobs 1991 / Jordan–Jacobs 1994) — Gaussian gating inside mixtures of experts; the classical ancestry; no negative model, no support threshold.
- **CN-DPM** (Lee et al., ICLR 2020) — generative mixture density detects the current data mode to gate modular growth in task-free CL; vision, module assignment, not per-matrix adapter-vs-base.
- **GRACE / WISE / TokenMem** — activation-keyed deferral, side-memory routing, per-layer zero-init gates: single-layer/fact-editing or learned-discriminative; the paper itself cites GRACE+WISE.
- **MoE-LoRA CL family** (MILE, MoCLoRA, DynMoLE, MoLE...) — all learned-discriminative routers; weight-space orthogonality methods (O-LoRA, InfLoRA, OP-LoRA) are a different axis and refuted at scale by the paper's own ρ_k measurement.
- **Internal cousins:** Research 141 (C-LoRA multi-LoRA dispatch — CONDITIONAL GAIN, dispatch-count kernel, no gating), Research 161 (dMoE block-level routing — token/action granularity, learned router), Research 62 (SHINE context-to-LoRA — generation, not gating), ASFT (train-time anchor — the complement).

## Fusion

**paper × `CorpusDistanceGate` × `contrastive_scope` × `LoRAHotSwap` = the support-gated adapter stack.** None alone gives it: CorpusDistanceGate is one-sided fixed-threshold exemplar matching; contrastive_scope is token-count discrete; LoRAHotSwap has no input-conditioned admission; the paper's gate alone is a Python training-loop artifact. The fusion: a Rust modelless gate primitive + the existing adapter registry + the vessel/freeze container = per-phase routed specialists with offline-certifiable excess bounds — a capability (bounded-memory adaptive specialists that provably preserve off-support behavior) none of the four has alone.

## GOAT gate (primitive)

- **G1 correctness:** frozen-activation-corpus decision identity vs a reference implementation (log-space tol); EM determinism — same input bytes → bit-identical artifact (BLAKE3 pin); empty/unfitted gate = closed (abstain-always, the `CorpusDistanceGate` empty-corpus precedent).
- **G2 perf (restated per verdict round 1 — the projection is counted):** per-REQUEST consumers (reflex abstain, healer scope): one JL projection + one GMM-pair eval per request on small embeddings — assert a wall-clock bar vs the request's own decision pass (≤ a few %). Per-MATRIX adapter gating: the honest budget is the projection (D×E, same order as the gated multiply at encoder widths) + ~50–64K FLOPs of GMM evals at K=32/E=256 — assert ≤ 15% added latency vs the ungated adapter path and PIN the q/k/v gate-sharing (−43% gate count) as the cost-spreading mechanism. Zero-alloc hot path; `black_box`-defended timed regions (the loud-zero rules).
- **G3 no-regression:** disarmed path byte-identical; paired non-inferiority vs fixed-threshold `CorpusDistanceGate` on the abstain benchmark (LB95 ≥ 0), positive where the fixed gate is miscalibrated (planted miscalibration arm).
- **G4 alloc:** counting-allocator canary; `Vec::with_capacity` once, scratch reuse.
- **G5 certification instrument:** excess measured on planted off-domain probes ≤ bound-predicted volume within slack; **the instrument REDs on a deliberately leaky discriminator-class gate** (two-sided canary, the §3.6 discipline).

## Honest weak points (named first for the verdict)

1. **The retention win is demonstrated on in-model LoRA at ≥1.5B decoder scale.** Our fastest consumers (reflex abstain, healer scope) use the gate WITHOUT any adapter — pure input-distribution abstention, where the paper's evidence transfers only partially (the OOD-open-rate measurement does transfer; the retention claim does not). The gated-adapter consumer is a future lane gated on Plan 439's pilot.
2. **One v1 preprint, one model family (Qwen2.5), zero citations yet** — treat the 0.5B bracket in Plan 439 as independent replication; a mismatch is the answer.
3. **Component-level prior art is dense** (RMD, LR-OOD, Heng-Soh, HME, CN-DPM) — the claim is the scoped conjunction, not "first density gate".
